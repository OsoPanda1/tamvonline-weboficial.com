# Community Participation Guidelines

These are initial participation expectations for contributions to the TAMV Online Network repository. They are not a claim that a separately incorporated community body or formal adjudication process already exists.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with respect and critique ideas, evidence, and code rather than attacking people.
- Attribute work accurately and respect licenses, privacy, and intellectual property.
- Be specific when reporting defects: provide reproducible steps and relevant sources.
- State uncertainty and limitations instead of inventing facts, credentials, affiliations, metrics, or test results.
- Keep discussions accessible and relevant to the repository's purpose.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intentional publication of private information, deceptive attribution, and knowingly fabricated evidence are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a concern through the repository's available private channels when the issue involves sensitive personal data or safety. Use public issues for non-sensitive technical and editorial matters only. The maintainer may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations or create legal, privacy, or security risk.
